Last updated: October 4, 2026

In short: your period, cycle and health logs are stored only on your phone. You don’t need an account; these logs are never sent to us or any other server, never sold and never used for advertising.

1. Who we are

MyLuna – Period Tracker (“the App”) is developed by Nedim Baysoz. Questions: nedimbaysoz27@icloud.com

2. Data that stays on your device

The following information you enter is kept only on your device, in the App’s protected storage:

  • Period days, flow, symptoms, mood, discharge, sex, medication, test results, basal temperature, weight, water, sleep, exercise and notes
  • Cycle settings, reminder preferences, birth control method, pregnancy details
  • Your first name, if you choose to add it

We cannot access this data. For your privacy it is also excluded from iCloud and Google phone backups. Reminders are scheduled on your device and never sent to a server.

3. Apple Health and Health Connect

With MyLuna Pro you can choose to sync period days, basal body temperature and ovulation tests with Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android), and Apple Watch wrist temperature can be read. This only happens with your permission and entirely on your device. Data from health apps is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and is never shared with third parties.

4. Backups and sharing

Backup files, the doctor report (PDF) and the partner summary leave your device only when you create and share them, and go where you choose (e.g. Files, e-mail). Backups can be password-encrypted (AES-256).

5. Ads

The App shows no ads and shares no data with ad networks. If ads are added to the free version in the future, this policy will be updated first.

6. Purchases

MyLuna Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play; your payment details are handled by Apple or Google and are never seen by us. The App only learns from the store whether your subscription is active and stores that on the device.

7. Sharing

We never share or sell your health and cycle data. Apart from Apple and Google Play for purchases, no data is passed to third parties. Your health logs are not held by us, so they cannot be disclosed even under a legal request.

8. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Use Settings → App settings → “Delete all data” to erase everything permanently, or uninstall the App. To cancel a subscription, use your App Store / Google Play subscription settings.

9. Your rights (GDPR and Turkish KVKK)

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to object to processing and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because your health data is only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly in the App.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

11. Security

The App offers a Face ID / Touch ID / passcode lock, discreet notifications and a discreet app icon. With the lock on, the App is hidden in the app switcher.

12. Medical disclaimer

MyLuna is not a medical device. Predictions are statistical estimates; they must not be used as contraception or instead of medical advice.

13. Changes and contact

When we update this policy the date above changes. Contact: nedimbaysoz27@icloud.com
