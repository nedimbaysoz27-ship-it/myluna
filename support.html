Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

All your period and health logs are stored only on your phone. There is no account and your logs are never sent to a server.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Create a backup file (optionally password-protected) under Settings → Backup and import, then open it on the new phone with “Restore from backup”. For privacy your logs are not included in iCloud/Google backups, so regular backups are recommended.

Can I switch from another app?

Yes. Under Settings → Backup and import → “Import from another app”, choose your export file or paste your period start dates.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open Settings → “Go Pro” and tap “Restore purchases”. You need to be signed in with the App Store / Google Play account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MyLuna. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

How accurate are the predictions?

Predictions are based on your recent cycles and get sharper as you log a few. Ovulation tests and basal temperature improve them. MyLuna is not a method of contraception.

I don’t get notifications.

Check that notifications for MyLuna are allowed in your phone settings and that the reminder is turned on under Settings → Notifications and reminders.

I can’t unlock the app.

The lock uses your phone’s Face ID / Touch ID or passcode. If the phone’s screen lock is removed, the app lock turns off automatically.
